The small backend behind Adieu.

## What it is

`adieu-app` lets anyone scan their mailbox for forgotten accounts, for free. Adieu also plans a paid service that closes those accounts on your behalf — that part isn't live yet.

Right now, this API's only job is to collect the email address of anyone who wants to be notified when the paid service launches.

## Status

Early, pre-release, intentionally minimal.
